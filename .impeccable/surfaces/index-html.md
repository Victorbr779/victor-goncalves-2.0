## Direction contract

THESIS: A circuit theater built for a one-person web studio: the page itself is a lit, engineered instrument proving the craft it sells, refusing the dark-SaaS hero with glass cards stacked on gradient orbs — here every glow traces a connected circuit, never a free-floating blob.

OWN-WORLD: Continuous near-black canvas (#05060f); headings fill with an icy #d8ecf8→#98c0ef gradient and a soft cool glow; body copy stays flat, muted blue-gray. Surfaces are barely-there glass (1–6% blue wash) with a cool inset upper rim, 16–28px radii. Buttons are small pill capsules with a 6% blue wash and an inset ring. Circuit hairlines and tiny registration-dot emitters (≤9px) tie headings, the portrait frame, and project windows into one visual system. Manrope carries headings, Inter carries body and labels.

STORY: The visitor recognizes within seconds a technical, premium operator — the circuitry proves engineering care the way the previous lit sign proved craftsmanship — and converts by filling the WhatsApp form, with the lit capsule button leading every section back to it.
